# Kafene

> Kafene is a New York-based point-of-sale financing platform offering transparent
> lease-to-own (LTO) purchase options to prime and non-prime consumers at retail
> checkout. Founded in 2019, it underwrites shoppers with an AI-driven risk model
> drawing on 20,000+ data points, returning approvals up to $5,000 in seconds, then
> pays the merchant directly on delivery confirmation while the consumer pays weekly
> or biweekly until ownership.

Kafene publishes **no public developer program**. As of 2026-08-01 there is no
OpenAPI, GraphQL, AsyncAPI or MCP contract, no SDKs or client libraries in any public
package registry, no GitHub organization, and no `/.well-known/` discovery surface on
any kafene.com host. Merchant integration (in-store POS origination and text-to-apply)
is arranged directly with Kafene's partner team, and Kafene also reaches merchants
through financing-waterfall networks such as Versatile Credit and LendPro. The
`api.kafene.com` hostname resolves to Cloudflare but has no live origin (HTTP 530,
error 1016). Do not assume an API exists; contact Kafene to arrange an integration.
